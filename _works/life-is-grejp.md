lead: The body is given up to the worlds of cyberspace. With the material gone, a new virtual identity appears — an artificial intelligence that, through the immortality of the virtual, reaches past the limits of a biological body, and dualisms such as mind/body, reality/illusion, creator/created and man/woman fall away. Not a dystopian or utopian future, but an intimate, scenic picture of the present that embodies primary human drives and needs.
