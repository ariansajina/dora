lead: >-
  Three artists look at what moving into online space does to the body —
  face filters, selfie cameras, live streams, staged and manipulated photographs —
  and at how the images we share take on a life of their own. Brkarić showed
  Striptease: the video she shot of herself with a phone camera and flash at
  Marsovo polje, alongside the set and costumes of the stage performance, which
  had been buried at Marsovo polje and dug up again for the exhibition.
